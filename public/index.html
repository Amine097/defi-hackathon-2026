One position, a dozen silent dependencies.

A Pendle principal token posted on Morpho looks like one line in a portfolio. Underneath it sit five layers, plus the things nobody lists: the issuer, the stablecoin it is accounted in, the provider behind the oracle feed. FAULTLINE reads them live from Pendle, Morpho, DefiLlama and Ethereum itself.

Stay, exit, or hold to maturity.

Exiting is certain: slippage, fees and the yield you give up. Staying is cheap and uncertain. FAULTLINE puts both on one scale with honest ranges, says how wrong the estimate would have to be to change the answer, and says Review when it cannot make a firm call.

Arm. Defend. Prove.

A borrower authorises a narrow policy in advance: when to act, how much, and what is never allowed. A keeper watches the oracle, picks the cheapest action that is safe, executes it without a new signature, and verifies the result on-chain.
