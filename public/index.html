How it works: five layers of dependency

L1

Settlement chain

The chain the position settles on: halts, reorgs, congestion.

L2

Yield source & issuer

The underlying asset (e.g. sUSDe) and who issues it (Ethena): depeg, funding risk.

L3

Oracle

The price feed that values the asset: manipulation or stale prices.

L4

PT market & AMM curve

Where the PT trades and exits: liquidity depth and slippage.

L5

Lending market

Morpho/Aave using it as collateral: liquidation cascades.

Methodology & assumptions
  • Mock numbers: failure probabilities and loss-given-failure values are placeholders over a 30-day horizon until real data discovery lands.
  • Monte Carlo: 20,000 simulations; each node fails independently with its probability.
  • Transmission: a failure propagates along dependency edges (A depends on B) scaled by a per-edge transmission factor.
  • Exit cost: slippage approximation from exit-pool liquidity plus gas; certain, paid immediately.
  • Decision: STAY if expected loss from staying is below the certain exit cost, otherwise EXIT.
