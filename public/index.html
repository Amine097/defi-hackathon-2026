How it works

Five layers of dependency, from the chain up to the lending market.

The five layers FaultLine traces
L1

Settlement chain

The chain the position settles on: halts, reorgs, congestion.

L2

Yield source & issuer

The underlying asset and who issues it: depeg, funding risk.

L3

Oracle

The price feed that values the asset: manipulation or stale prices.

L4

PT market & AMM

Where the PT trades and exits: liquidity depth and slippage.

L5

Lending market

Morpho/Aave using it as collateral: liquidation cascades.

Methodology & assumptions
  • Data: the position is a real Pendle PT used as Morpho Blue collateral, discovered live from the Pendle API, Morpho GraphQL, DefiLlama and Ethereum RPC. The position size is an input (default $1M). The badge at the top says whether numbers are live, a saved real snapshot, replayed from cache, or MOCK.
  • Failure priors (30-day horizon): DefiLlama hack history for the protocol cohort (TVL ≥ $10M), adjusted by audits / TVL / age; stablecoins use their own DefiLlama price history. Loss-given-failure and edge transmissions are stated assumptions (see each node's source in the X-Ray).
  • Expected loss: central value 1 − ∏(1 − p × loss-if-fails × path transmission), exact only if failures are independent and travel along their best path; it is bracketed by dependence-free bounds and by a low/high parameter band (each parameter is classed observed / calibrated / assumption). A 20,000-draw Monte Carlo gives the VaR95.
  • Exit side: executable Pendle SDK quote for selling the full PT size into the loan asset, re-executed on-chain with eth_call at a pinned block (no transaction sent), plus gas; plus the PT excess carry forgone (PT implied APY − USDC supply APY on the same Morpho market). Without a quote a constant-product approximation is used and labelled as such.
  • Breaking point: only for a leveraged position. With ?borrower=0x… or auto a real Morpho borrower is read on-chain (debt, health factor, liquidation price) and stressed through Morpho's liquidation formula. It is a conditional price move, not a probability. Without debt the position has no liquidation threshold and the panel says so.
  • Scenarios: losses are conditional on the shock happening (probability 1), not probability-weighted; a correlated depeg + oracle + liquidity scenario is included.
  • Decision: EXIT if even the low staying loss exceeds the high exit cost; STAY if even the high staying loss is below the low exit cost; otherwise REVIEW, with the point comparison shown as a conditional lean. Stale (quote > 60 s, chain state > 120 s), cached or approximated inputs force REVIEW.
  • Exit runway: exit cost alone vs under stress, stuck share, trend and time left come from a separate backend endpoint. When it is not connected the card says so; no number is estimated in the browser.
