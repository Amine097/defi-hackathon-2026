Vault Risk Analyzer

Paste a Morpho vault (V1 MetaMorpho or V2) on Ethereum. FaultLine reads it on-chain at a pinned block, maps every underlying market, collateral and oracle, prices the expected loss of staying against the real cost of withdrawing, and gives a decision. All figures come from the backend; nothing is precomputed.
