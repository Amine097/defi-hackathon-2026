Vault intelligence

Paste a Morpho vault. FAULTLINE reads it on-chain at one pinned block, prices what staying could lose against what leaving costs, and tells you to stay, exit or review.

Ethereum mainnet only. Supports Morpho V1 (MetaMorpho) and V2 vaults. The exposure is hypothetical: no wallet is connected and nothing is signed.

Or start from a known vault

Each example runs a fresh live analysis with the exposure and horizon above.

    Details

    Run your own hypothetical shock

    Pick one dependency and how hard it fails. FAULTLINE re-runs the full analysis live with that shock (15–25 s). The result is a hypothesis, not a forecast.

    Severity is how hard it fails, from 0% to 100%: for collateral the price drop, for an oracle the pricing error, for a market the share lost.
