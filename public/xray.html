How to read the X-Ray

  1. The graph is your position's hidden dependency stack. The orange hexagon is the position. Each arrow A → B means "A depends on B". Colours are the 5 layers: chain, yield source, oracle, AMM market, lending.
  2. Make something fail. Click any node (or a scenario on the right, e.g. "Oracle failure"). The red wave shows how the failure travels to the position, hop by hop.
  3. Read the verdict on the right. Stay costs an uncertain expected loss; Exit costs the execution (slippage + fees + gas) plus the extra yield you give up (carry). Both sides carry an uncertainty band: EXIT or STAY only when the bands do not overlap and the inputs are fresh, otherwise REVIEW with a lean. The margin compares central values (loss − exit side incl. carry).
  4. Stress-test it. Use the Break-even sliders to see at which incident probability or exit cost the verdict flips.
